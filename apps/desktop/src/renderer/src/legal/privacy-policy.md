# ScholarSeed Privacy Policy

**English** · [简体中文](privacy-policy_zh.md)

**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

**Effective date: September 22, 2026**

---

This policy explains how ScholarSeed (the "Product") collects, uses, stores,
and protects your personal information. The Product is operated by Alibaba
DAMO Academy (Hangzhou) Technology Co., Ltd. ("we" or "us"). Please read this
policy in full.

By confirming acceptance on the page or using the Product, you agree to this
policy and its subsequent revisions. If you disagree, stop using the Product.

## 1. Information we collect and store

1.1 **Account information.** We collect the mobile phone number or email address
you provide at registration.

1.2 **Inference relay data.** In signed-in mode, when you use an AI model, our
servers relay your inputs, such as conversation instructions, to a third-party
model such as Qwen for inference. We may store relayed conversation queries to
analyze and improve the product experience, but not to train models.

1.3 **Search metadata.** This includes fields involved in search requests,
such as search terms and query timestamps.

## 2. Purposes of use

We use the collected information to:

- Provide AI inference relay and search services to signed-in users;
- Analyze conversation data to improve the product experience, without using
  the contents of original files uploaded by users;
- Maintain account security and system stability;
- Respond to feedback and technical support requests;
- Fulfill obligations under laws and regulations.

## 3. Storage and protection

3.1 **Location.** Your personal information is stored primarily on servers in China.

3.2 **Retention.** We retain your information only for the shortest period
needed for business purposes or legal requirements. After that period, we delete
or anonymize it.

3.3 **Security.** We use safeguards such as encrypted transmission and strict
access controls to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure,
or loss.

## 4. Information sharing

4.1 We do not disclose your personal information publicly for commercial purposes
or sell it.

4.2 Information may be provided to third parties only in these circumstances:

- **Infrastructure providers:** To provide necessary functions such as cloud
  services or model inference relay, for example Qwen;
- **Model inference context:** When you use AI features, our servers relay your
  conversation instructions to a third-party model API provider such as Qwen.
  We may store those instructions for product improvement analysis, but not
  for model training;
- **Affiliates:** When necessary to fulfill obligations under this agreement;
- **Legal requirements:** In response to lawful requests from judicial or
  administrative authorities.

## 5. Your rights

- **Access and correction:** You can access and correct your account and personal
  information.
- **Deletion:** You can request deletion of your personal information.
- **Account closure:** You may close your account at any time; we will then
  delete or anonymize your information.
- **Withdrawal of consent:** You can manage the scope of authorization in
  the Product settings.

## 6. Protection of minors

6.1 **The Product is primarily intended for adults.** We do not intentionally
collect personal information from children under 14.

6.2 **Users under 14** may not register an account or use signed-in mode. If a
guardian inadvertently provided a child's information, contact us for deletion.

6.3 **Minors aged 14 through 17** should use the Product with the presence and
consent of their guardian, who should supervise that use.

## 7. Policy updates

7.1 We may revise this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material
changes by means such as an in-product notice or an announcement on the official
website.

7.2 Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
