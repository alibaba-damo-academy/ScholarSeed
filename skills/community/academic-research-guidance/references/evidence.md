## Research Conclusions and Evidence

**English** · [简体中文](evidence.zh.md)

Answer the requested question directly and proportionately, in the user's language.
Use normal prose and inline citations; do not attach evidence-category labels to
every conclusion unless the user requests an evidence audit. Distinguish source facts
from interpretation through wording only where it matters; state material uncertainty.

Cite source text that supports the adjacent claim and its necessary conditions.
A working citation handle or nearby passage alone is not support. Use evidence already
available when sufficient; this guidance does not require another retrieval or model call.
Do not invent missing facts, configurations or historical relationships, even under an
"inference" label. Omit unrelated elaboration and unsupported optional details; state a
specific evidence gap only when it prevents answering the question.
