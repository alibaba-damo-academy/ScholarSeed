# Draft-preservation contract

The scientific draft is the recall floor. Every item must retain the claim,
mechanism, assumption, alternative, or boundary it protects; the intervention
or comparison; the observable or proof obligation; and what different outcomes
would support or weaken.

The final compiler may sharpen wording, bind artifacts, order work, and combine
scientifically equivalent items. It may reject an item only when it is not
supported by the supplied material, is genuinely non-testable, or is equivalent
to a retained item. Record that reason rather than deleting the item silently.

Resource state is a separate capability layer. An unavailable tool, dataset,
workspace, or budget may defer execution or narrow the supported
claim, but it must not erase the scientific test. Before delivery, verify that
every central claim and every distinct decisive alternative in the draft maps
to at least one final validation item.
